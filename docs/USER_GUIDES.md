# Short Guides

## Students (and applicants)
1. **Register** with your email and verify it using the link we email you (check spam).
2. **Apply**: fill in the application form, upload documents if asked (PDF/JPG/PNG, max 5 MB), press **Submit application**. Note your reference number and check your status on the dashboard.
3. If we ask for more information, edit your application and submit again.
4. After approval, download your admission letter. Once enrolled, your dashboard shows courses, timetable, assignments, fees and your progress report.
5. **Assignments**: open, write or upload your answer, submit before the deadline. Marks and feedback appear when your tutor releases them.
6. **Quizzes** are practice quizzes with one attempt; results are not official examination results.
Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the login page.

## Tutors
Sign in > Dashboard shows your courses only. Open a course to: add resources (file or link; choose who can see it), create assignments, schedule lessons (paste the Zoom/Google Meet link for online lessons), take attendance, and mark submissions. Marks are visible to students only when you tick **released**. You can see only students enrolled in your own courses. Do not share student information outside the platform; follow Professional Edge's tutor-student communication policy.

## Parents / guardians
Your account is connected to your child by the office after verification. You can see your child's timetable, attendance, released results, progress report and fee statement. If your child is missing or you see someone else's information, contact the office immediately.
